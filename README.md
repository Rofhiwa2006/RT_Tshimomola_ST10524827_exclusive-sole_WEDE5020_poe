##PART ONE DETAILS: 
Exclusive Sole is a modern sneaker and streetwear business created for customers who are passionate about footwear, fashion and personal style. The index.html page is the home page of the website and introduces Exclusive Sole, its brand identity, slogan “Step Different. Stay Exclusive.” and the products and services offered. The aboutus.html page provides information about the business, including its story, vision, mission and the founder and owner, Rofhiwa Theophilus Tshimomola, who created Exclusive Sole from a passion for sneakers, fashion and street culture. The product.html page displays the different footwear products available, including lifestyle sneakers, premium sneakers, casual footwear, mules and limited drops, with information such as product names, prices, sizes, colours, conditions and availability. The services.html page explains the services offered by Exclusive Sole, including sneaker sales, product sourcing, streetwear styling, special product drops, giveaways and promotional campaigns. The inquiry.html page allows customers to send inquiries about products they are interested in by providing details such as their name, contact information, preferred product, shoe size, colour, budget and any additional requests. The contact.html page provides customers with ways to communicate with Exclusive Sole about products, prices, sizes, availability and general questions, including the business WhatsApp number 071 393 8987 and TikTok for updates. Together, these pages create a complete website that introduces the Exclusive Sole brand, showcases its products, explains its services, provides information about the business, allows customers to make inquiries and gives customers a way to get in contact with the business. The website uses a consistent red, black and white colour scheme to create a strong, clean and modern streetwear style while representing the brand's focus on confidence, individuality, quality and affordability.

##PART TWO DETAILS

The Exclusive Sole project is a sneaker and streetwear online business website created to promote and showcase stylish sneakers and fashion products. The main purpose of the project is to give customers an easy way to learn about the business, view available products and services, make enquiries, and find contact information. The website is designed using HTML and CSS, with all pages sharing the same red, white and black colour theme to create a strong, modern and streetwear-inspired brand identity. The website consists of several pages, including index.html, which serves as the home page and introduces Exclusive Sole; product.html, which displays the sneakers and products available; services.html, which explains the services offered by the business; aboutus.html, which provides information about the business, its purpose and its founder; inquiry.html, which allows customers to make product or service enquiries; and contact.html, which provides the business contact details so customers can get in touch. The project also focuses on creating a professional and user-friendly layout that works across different screen sizes. Overall, Exclusive Sole is designed to build a strong online presence for the sneaker business, attract customers, promote products, support customer communication and present the brand as a modern streetwear and sneaker store.
