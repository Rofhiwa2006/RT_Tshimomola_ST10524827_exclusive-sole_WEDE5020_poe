About Exclusive Sole

Who We Are

Exclusive Sole was started right here in Sandton, South Africa. We wanted to solve a major problem for local sneakerheads: fake shoes and long shipping times from overseas!

Every shoe we sell is physically inspected by hand. We check box labels, stitching, materials, and blacklight stamps so you never have to worry about buying fakes.
